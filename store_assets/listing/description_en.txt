DukApp is the app of the Keresztény Testvéri Közösség community: manage your buffet balance, community jobs and community goals in one place.

BALANCE AND BUFFET
• See your balance and your latest purchases at a glance.
• Top up with a few taps – the amount is credited instantly.
• Send money to other members of the community.
• Your own barcode is always at hand.

JOBS
• Browse upcoming jobs and sign up with one tap – you only see the jobs you can join by age and gender.
• Join the waiting list when a job is full; a freed place automatically goes to the first person waiting.
• Sign up your spouse or your children too.
• Week and month calendar views, and export to your own calendar.
• Every job has its own chat with the participants and the person in charge.
• Shareable links: tap a link to go straight to the job.

STATUS
• Follow your own and the community's monthly goals by round and season.

LIVE BROADCASTS
• Watch the community's live broadcasts and choose the language you want to listen to.
• Audio-only mode: listen with the screen off, even in your pocket.

NOTIFICATIONS
• New jobs, chat messages, top-ups and reminders – switch each kind on or off separately.

EASY SIGN-IN
• Sign in with your e-mail address, a Google account or an Apple account.
• Available in Hungarian and English.

The app is made for the members of the community. Questions or suggestions: support@bcc-ktk.org
