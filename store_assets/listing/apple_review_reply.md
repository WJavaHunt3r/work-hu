## Guideline 3.2 - Business

DukApp is intended for a general public audience, and we have chosen public distribution on purpose. Anyone can download the app and create an account without an invitation, approval or any affiliation with an organization.

DukApp is the app of the Keresztény Testvéri Közösség (BCC KTK), a Christian community in Hungary. Some of its screens, such as the member status, the jobs and the live broadcasts of the community, are only shown to people the community has registered as members. All of those are hidden for an ordinary account, which is why your review account only shows the Balance and Profile screens. These two screens are the parts of the app for the general public.

**1. Is the app restricted to users who are part of a single company or organization?**
No. Registration is open to everyone. The community's own members get additional screens, but the account, the balance and the profile do not require any membership.

**2. Is the app designed for use by a limited or specific group of companies or organizations?**
No. The app is not a business-to-business or employee app. It is one app for individual people, and there are no clients, partners or organizations that sign up.

**3. What features are intended for the general public?**
These are available to every user without any affiliation:
- Creating an account (e-mail and password, Sign in with Google, or Sign in with Apple).
- A personal balance that the user can top up with a card and use to pay for food and drinks at the community's buffet [confirm: where it is used], and that can be sent to other users.
- A personal barcode.
- A profile with name, e-mail and phone number, notification settings and language (Hungarian or English).
- In-app account deletion, and the deletion page at https://dukapp.bcc-ktk.org/delete-account.

**4. How do users obtain an account?**
By registering in the app themselves, with no invitation code, pre-approval or administrator needed. The registration form asks for name, gender, e-mail address and a password, or the user signs in with a Google or Apple account. People who are members of the community are later marked as members by an administrator, which unlocks the member screens.

**5. Is there any paid content in the app, and who pays for it?**
No. The app sells no digital content, subscriptions or features, and has no in-app purchases. The only payment is the optional top-up of the user's own balance by card, handled by our payment processor SumUp. The balance is used for physical goods consumed outside of the app (food and drinks at the buffet). Nobody pays to create an account or to use a feature.

If you still consider the app to be limited to a specific organization, please tell us which part of the answers above leads to that conclusion, and we will gladly clarify.

Thank you. You are right: after signing in with Apple, a new account was sent to a "choose a new password" screen that is meant for accounts created with a temporary password. ## Background audio

The app plays the community's live broadcasts, and has an audio-only mode for listening to a broadcast without the picture. People use it to follow a service with the phone locked or in a pocket, so the audio has to keep playing in the background, which is why the app declares the audio background mode. It is only used for this live audio (nothing plays in the background otherwise), and the lock-screen controls (play and pause) are shown while it plays.

To see it with the demo account:
1. Sign in with the demo account and open the **Live** tab while a broadcast is on the air [see the broadcast times above].
2. Switch on **Audio only**.
3. Press the side button to lock the iPhone, or go to the Home Screen. The audio keeps playing, and the lock screen shows the playback controls.
